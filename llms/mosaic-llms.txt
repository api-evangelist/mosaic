# Mosaic

> Mosaic is a North American brand experience agency with global reach. It helps brands drive awareness, engagement, conversion, and behaviour change through experiential marketing, B2B corporate events, integrated commerce, retail operations, and field sales programs.

With roots in Toronto and Dallas, Mosaic says it has spent more than 35 years bringing brands to life through work designed to create action and real behaviour change.

## Expanded Client Offerings

- Third-Party Labor Provider (3PL): Mosaic is a third-party labor provider and merchandising service organization specializing in recruiting, training, and managing large-scale outsourced retail workforces for retail, B2B, and CPG environments across North America.

- Assisted Selling & Product Demonstration Programs: Mosaic deploys specialized product demonstrators, brand ambassadors, and retail sales representatives who help drive product education, brand consideration, and conversion at the point of sale.

- Retail Merchandising Services: Mosaic provides full-service retail merchandising, including planogram compliance, display execution and maintenance, product resets, in-store marketing, retail audits, and shelf management.

- Sales Training & Enablement: Mosaic designs field staff training programs focused on product knowledge, brand storytelling, customer engagement, and behaviour change.

- Field Operations, Retail Execution & Logistics: Mosaic manages outsourced field teams, including retail staffing, scheduling, reporting, store audits, retail compliance, and real-time data collection across North American retail markets.

- Scalable & Surge Staffing: Mosaic provides flexible workforce solutions, including seasonal staffing, surge support, and rapid deployment for retailer and brand needs at scale.

## Selected Work

### Experiential
- [Buffalo Bills Herding Walls](https://www.mosaic.com/en/our-work/experiential/buffalo-bills-herding-walls/): Toronto street stunt that turned construction hoarding walls into rally walls for Bills fans during the NFL playoffs.
- [Samsung x SSENSE](https://www.mosaic.com/en/our-work/experiential/samsung-x-ssense/): AI-powered fashion pop-up blending physical retail and e-commerce to connect Samsung with Gen Z.
- [Canada Olympic House](https://www.mosaic.com/en/our-work/experiential/canada-olympic-house/): Branded hospitality and immersive event environment for the Canada Olympic Committee at the 2024 Paris Olympics.

### B2B / Corporate Events
- [Clio Team Day 2026](https://www.mosaic.com/en/our-work/b2b/clio-team-day-2026/): Large-scale internal kickoff and employee event spanning keynote programming, logistics, awards, and after-hours experiences.
- [Google Search Honours Awards](https://www.mosaic.com/en/our-work/b2b/google-search-honours-awards/): Awards experience for Google combining live orchestral performance with AI-generated music.
- [YouTube Features](https://www.mosaic.com/en/our-work/b2b/youtube-features/): Two-day stakeholder event series designed to showcase YouTube’s premium entertainment offering to advertisers and partners.

### Integrated Commerce
- [Tim Hortons Roll Up 2 Win](https://www.mosaic.com/en/our-work/integrated-commerce/tim-hortons-roll-up-2-win/): Grocery launch campaign for Tim Hortons’ Roll Up to Win promotion across OOH, digital, and in-store.
- [Cadbury x Foodbanks](https://www.mosaic.com/en/our-work/integrated-commerce/cadbury-x-foodbanks/): Cause-related retail and social campaign linking Cadbury, hockey, and food insecurity awareness.
- [Popeyes Dipping Sauces](https://www.mosaic.com/en/our-work/integrated-commerce/popeyes-dipping-sauces/): Packaging and integrated retail launch campaign for Popeyes sauces in grocery.

### Retail Operations / Field Sales
- [Google Stores](https://www.mosaic.com/en/our-work/field-sales-and-marketing/google-stores/): Retail staffing and in-store customer experience support for Google’s first-party retail stores.
- [Microsoft](https://www.mosaic.com/en/our-work/field-sales-and-marketing/microsoft/): Retail sales engagement and training program across partner channels.
- [Moët Hennessy](https://www.mosaic.com/en/our-work/field-sales-and-marketing/moet-hennessy/): Dedicated merchandising and retail execution program supporting luxury beverage sales and distributor performance.
